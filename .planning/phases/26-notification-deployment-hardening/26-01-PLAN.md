These correspond to the Pydantic Settings fields in bot/config.py:
- schedule_grpc_host (default: "schedule-service") -> SCHEDULE_GRPC_HOST

- mini_app_url (default: "https://t.me/RutTrackBot/checkin") -> MINI_APP_URL

Pydantic BaseSettings reads env vars case-insensitively by default, but UPPER_CASE is the docker-compose convention.
